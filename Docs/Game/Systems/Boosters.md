# Boosters

> Summary: The four boosters under the board (Freeze, Rocket, Hammer, Vacuum; the Rocket comes with its own session), their saved stock with a free starting amount, the buy popup that trades coins for a pack, and the flow every aimed booster follows: aim with the screen dimmed around the board, tap a target, the stock goes down, the booster's shot plays, the board's views catch up. What a booster does to the board is a logic effect; this feature only presents it.

## Code
- Folder: `Assets/Game/Logic/Boosters/` (stock and purchase), `Assets/Game/Logic/Play/Effects/` (effects), `Assets/Game/Data/Boosters/` (the saved stock), `Assets/Game/Runtime/Play/` (`Scripts/Services/` the boosters and the store, `Scripts/Presentation/Boosters/` slots, overlay and shots, `Scripts/Settings/` definitions and motion, `Scripts/UI/BoosterPurchasePopup.cs`; assets in `Settings/` and `Prefabs/UI/`)
- Assembly: `Game.Logic`, `Game.Data`, `Game`
- Entry points: `PlaySession.Use(booster, overlay, shots)` and `PlaySession.IsAvailable(booster)` ([Play](Play.md)), called from `LevelHud`; `BoosterInventory`, `BoosterPurchase` (`Logic/Boosters/`); `IBooster` (`Play/Scripts/Services/IBooster.cs`) with `TargetedBooster` and `FreezeBooster`; `HammerEffect`, `VacuumEffect` (`Logic/Play/Effects/`); `BoosterPurchasePopup` (`Play/Scripts/UI/`); the definitions `FreezeBooster.asset`, `HammerBooster.asset`, `VacuumBooster.asset` and `BoosterMotionSettings.asset` in `Play/Settings/`, registered by `PlayInstaller`

## How it works
- **Stock.** `new BoosterInventory(store, startingStock)` starts every booster at its free starting amount and puts the saved stock over it, so a booster nobody has touched keeps its starting amount. `Count(id)` reads a stock (0 for an id it does not know), `TryUse(id)` takes one and saves (false at zero, nothing saved), `Add(id, amount)` adds and saves. `IBoosterStore` is its port, declared in `Game.Logic` like the wallet's; `SavedBoosterStore` keeps all stocks in one Core Save document (`BoostersDocument` under `boosters`). Contract: `Save` returns once the stock is persisted. A negative stored stock or amount throws, as a programmer error.
- **Purchase.** `BoosterPurchase.TryBuy(id, amount, price)` checks the balance, adds the pack to the stock (saved) and then spends the coins through `CoinWallet.TrySpend` (saved), so a crash between the two writes leaves boosters not paid for rather than coins gone for nothing ([Decisions](../Decisions.md)). `CanAfford(price)` (the wallet's `CanSpend`) is what the popup shows. A negative price throws before anything is saved.
- **Definitions.** Each booster has a settings asset: `BoosterDefinition` holds the id, icon, title, one line on what it does (the buy popup and the aiming banner both show it), starting stock, pack size and price; `FreezeDefinition` adds the seconds, `TargetedDefinition` the shot prefab. Starting values: 2 free, 3 for 100 coins, 10 seconds of freeze.
- **Registration.** `PlayInstaller` registers the store, the inventory, the purchase, the motion settings and one `IBooster` per booster, in slot order, each through `RegisterBooster(builder, startingStock, definition, inventory => booster)`, which adds the definition's starting stock to the inventory's (a second booster with the same id throws) and registers the factory: `FreezeBooster(freeze)`, `TargetedBooster(hammer, new HammerEffect())`, `TargetedBooster(vacuum, new VacuumEffect())`. One line per booster, so a booster cannot be registered without its starting stock. The HUD resolves them as `IReadOnlyList<IBooster>`.
- **`IBooster`.** `Definition`; `IsAvailable(attempt)`; `Use(context, onDone)`; `Cancel()`. Contract: `IsAvailable` changes nothing and allocates nothing (the slots read it every frame); `Use` takes the attempt, the board interaction, the overlay and the shots from the `BoosterContext` and calls `onDone` exactly once when the use ends, however it ends (landed, cancelled, refused); the stock goes down only when the effect has been applied; `Cancel` ends a use still in progress (aiming, or a shot still playing) without firing and calls `onDone`, and does nothing when no use is in progress.
- **`TargetedBooster`** fixes the order of every aimed booster: release a held block (`BoardInteraction.Release`, since the attempt forgets it on `Aim`), `LevelAttempt.Aim`, show the overlay, board touches in `Pick` mode; on a tap, `LevelAttempt.TryApply(effect, x, y)`: a tap that is no target cancels; a hit takes one from the stock (saved), hides the overlay, puts the board in `Busy` and plays the definition's shot at the tapped cell (`LevelAttempt.CellAt`, the attempt's own rounding); when the shot lands, `BoardInteraction.CatchUp`, back to `Drag`, done. Each booster adds only its effect and its shot. It refuses to aim at zero stock.
- **`FreezeBooster`** has no target: `Use` freezes the attempt's timer (`LevelAttempt.Freeze`, refused while frozen, before the first touch and once decided) and takes one from the stock when it did; available when `LevelAttempt.CanFreeze` (boosters can be used and the time is not frozen), the same rule `Freeze` checks. Its use is over when `Use` returns, so its `Cancel` has nothing to end.
- **The way in.** A slot's press goes to the HUD (`LevelHud`): with stock, it calls `PlaySession.Use(booster, overlay, shots)` with its own overlay and shots; at zero, it calls the out-of-stock callback `GameEntry` gave its `Show`, which opens the buy popup ([Decisions](../Decisions.md)). `Use` ignores a press while a booster is in use (while aiming the slots are under the dim, and the overlay cancels) and otherwise starts the booster when `IsAvailable` (the session runs and the booster says so). `Start` and the level's end cancel any booster in use.
- **Effects.** `HammerEffect`: any block is a target, locked or not; `Apply` is `IBoard.Hit`, so an iced block loses one ice and stays. `VacuumEffect`: an unguarded block is a target; `Apply` collects every unguarded block of its color into a list made once per effect, then hits each, so the locks are read as they are at the tap (a block whose ice melts during the shot stays). Contracts of `IBoardEffect` in [Level attempt](LevelAttempt.md).
- **Slots.** `BoosterBar` on the HUD's booster row binds the slots placed in the HUD prefab, left to right, to the registered boosters and hides the slots left over (a horizontal layout keeps the shown ones centered). Every frame each `BoosterSlot` gets `PlaySession.IsAvailable` and the stock and changes its button, its look (half transparent when off) and its badge only when they changed: the stock on `Btn_circle_blue_small`, or a green "+" at zero. Slots are off before the level's first touch, once it is decided, and the Freeze while the time is frozen. The slots are placed in the prefab rather than made at run time because their buttons' press feedback is injected when Core makes the screen.
- **Aiming overlay.** `AimingOverlay` is a full-screen child of the HUD drawn over its content: four dim rectangles around the board's rectangle on screen (`BoardInteraction.ScreenRect`, the board's box with its frame projected), a banner with the booster's title, its line under it, and a copy of the active slot (its icon on the slot's art) placed over the real one. A press on any of them cancels (the overlay handles the press for its children); a press inside the board's rectangle reaches the board's collider and is a pick.
- **Shots.** `BoosterShots` (a full-screen child of the HUD over the overlay) makes each definition's shot prefab once and plays it from the active slot's position. `BoosterShot.Play(from, target, board, motion, onLanded)` kills the shot's tween still playing (the fade of the shot before), shows the shot and plays the sequence its subclass builds, ending hidden; the sequence is targeted at the shot and linked to its object, so a replay or a `Stop` ends it and an old sequence cannot hide a new shot. A subclass implements only `Build(from, target, board, motion, onLanded)`, contract: places its parts, returns a sequence that makes the views of the blocks the effect took vanish through `BoardInteraction.VanishGone` (or leaves them to `CatchUp`) and calls `onLanded` once, when the board may take touches again. `Stop` hides the shot without calling back. `HammerShot`: the hammer flies from its slot to above the tapped cell, winding up, strikes, the taken block shrinks into the struck cell, the hammer fades. `VacuumShot`: the vacuum grows above the tapped cell, the taken blocks float up to it one after the other, shrinking away, and the vacuum shrinks. Timings and eases are in `BoosterMotionSettings`; heights are in cells above the board.
- **Vanishing views.** `BoardInteraction.VanishGone(toward, lift, duration, stagger, ease)` makes every block view whose block is gone and still shown move to a point above a cell while shrinking to nothing (`BlockView.TryVanish`), one after the other; such a view counts as leaving, so `CatchUp` leaves it alone and the level does not end before it is gone ([Play](Play.md)). `BlockView.Init` puts the scale back, as pooled blocks are taken again.
- **Buy popup.** `BoosterPurchasePopup.Show(booster, canAfford, onBuy, onClose)`: the booster's title on the header ribbon, its icon and line in the inner box, a green "3 for 100" button (off and half transparent when the coins are short) and the corner close, from `PopupPanel` and `TextButtonGreen` ([Popups](Popups.md)). `GameEntry` pauses the session before opening it and resumes on either button; buying saves the stock and the coins, shows the new balance on the HUD's counter and closes; the player presses the booster again to use it.

## Adding the Rocket (session C)
- `RocketEffect : IBoardEffect` in `Logic/Play/Effects/` (`CanTarget`: any cell of a block; `Apply`: `Board.HitCell`).
- A `RocketShot : BoosterShot` prefab in `Play/Prefabs/UI/` that overrides `Build` (the rocket flies up from the bottom of the screen to the cell, then the views catch up with the split: `onLanded` and `TargetedBooster`'s `CatchUp`), with its timings in `BoosterMotionSettings`. `BoosterShot.Play` already kills a replayed shot's tween, hides it at the end and links it.
- A `TargetedDefinition` asset `RocketBooster.asset` (id `rocket`, `Rocket_Purple_Icon`, title, line, the shot), a `_rocket` field on `PlayInstaller` and one line between Freeze and Hammer: `RegisterBooster(builder, startingStock, _rocket, inventory => new TargetedBooster(_rocket, new RocketEffect(), inventory))`. The HUD's second slot then shows it.
- Crosshairs: a part of `AimingOverlay` that, for a booster that asks for them (a flag on its definition), marks every cell the effect can target (`CanTarget` on `LevelAttempt.Board`) with a tinted `SquareRoundedMask` at the cell's screen point (`BoardInteraction.ScreenPointOf`), shown with the overlay and hidden with it. Nothing else in this flow changes.

## Rules & invariants
- The stock goes down only when a shot lands, and is saved at once; a purchase saves the stock before the coins.
- The time keeps running while aiming and while a shot plays; the buy popup pauses it.
- While a shot plays the board takes no touches (`Busy`) and is not settled, so a level won by a shot ends after it.
- Slots and the overlay allocate nothing per frame.

## Tests
- `Tests/Boosters/BoosterInventoryTests`: starting stock, saved stock over it, unknown id, use lowers and saves, nothing at zero, add, negative amount, a new inventory starts where the last stopped, a negative saved stock throws.
- `Tests/Boosters/BoosterPurchaseTests`: a purchase adds the pack and spends the price, saves the stock before the coins, does nothing when short, throws on a negative price; `CanAfford`.
- `Tests/Coins/CoinWalletTests`: `CanSpend`; `TrySpend` lowers and saves, does nothing when short, throws on a negative amount.
- `Tests/Play/Effects/HammerEffectTests`, `VacuumEffectTests`: targets, a whole block taken, an iced block losing one ice, every unlocked block of the color taken, the locks read at the tap.
- `Tests/Play/Attempt/LevelTimerTests`, `LevelAttemptEffectTests`: a freeze stops the time for its seconds, is not used up while paused or before the start, is refused before the first touch, while frozen (`CanFreeze` false until it ends) and after the level failed.
- `Tests/Play/Settings/BoosterDefinitionsTests`: every definition has a distinct id, an icon, a title and a line; the HUD has a slot for every booster.
- `Tests/Play/Presentation/Blocks/BlockVanishTests`: a shown block starts leaving, one leaving or hidden does not, `Init` puts the scale back.
- Checked in the editor: before the first touch the slots are off; Freeze stops the countdown for 10 s and its slot is off meanwhile; Hammer dims around the board, the banner names it, the slot shows above the dim; a tap on a block flies the hammer there and the block shrinks away (an iced block loses one ice); a tap off the board, on another slot or on the active slot again cancels; Vacuum takes every unlocked block of the tapped color into the vacuum; a second hammer or vacuum used while the first one's shot still fades plays to its end and the board takes touches again (a shot is a component in the game assembly, which an EditMode test cannot attach a stand-in of); the last block taken by a booster wins after the shot; at zero stock the slot shows "+" and opens the buy popup, which pauses the time and spends the coins; restart while a shot plays.
