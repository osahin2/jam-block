## Phases
Each phase ends with compile, EditMode tests green, notes and index updated, a `--no-ff` merge into `develop`.

0. **Setup**: project, Core port, docs, skills.
1. **Scope and decisions** (with the developer): settle the open decisions below; download the supplied assets into `Assets/Art/` and review them (problems found go to the README feedback section); write the first entries in [Decisions](Decisions.md); create the four game assemblies and the test assembly.
2. **Data and logic** (`Game.Data`, `Game.Logic`): level format (board size, blocks with shape and color, doors with side, position, width and color, timer), board state, moves and collisions, exits through a matching door, win when the board is empty, fail when the timer reaches zero, coin wallet rules. EditMode tests first.
3. **Level editor** (`Game.Editor`): an editor window that draws the board; set width and height, paint blocks, place doors on the edges, set the timer; create, open, save, play from the editor; undo. Show the `LevelValidator` report (errors stop saving, warnings are shown). A test that every color id in the level assets has a material in the palette. Author the 5 levels with it (own designs, 4 x 5 to 5 x 6, two to six blocks; `Level_01` made again in the editor). Usability check: make a level from scratch without reading code. Agreed design:
   - **Window**: an `EditorWindow` drawing the board from above in 2D, UI Toolkit only (UXML, USS, C#), no IMGUI inside; if UI Toolkit blocks something, the whole window moves to IMGUI. Start with a spike the developer tries before the rest is built.
   - **Working copy**: the window edits a in-memory ScriptableObject copy (`DontSave`), undo works on it; Save validates and writes it into the `LevelAsset`, so a level with errors is never saved; `hasUnsavedChanges` asks on close. Update the "levels as ScriptableObjects" decision, which promises undo on the asset itself.
   - **Tools** as strategies (`Press`, `Drag`, `Release`, preview) looked up once, no switch: Select/Move (select a block or door, change its color, delete, drag to move), Block (one drag makes one block; a drag starting on a block extends it), Door (press on the edge strip and drag along it for the length), Erase.    - **Ice blocker and functional boosters**, agreed with the developer on 2026-11-21. Rules (after the reference, [Reference](Reference.md), "Boosters and ice"):
     - **Ice** is a number on a block. A block with ice left cannot move or leave. The ice drops by one for every block that leaves the board entirely, however it goes (through a door, by the hammer, by the vacuum, by the rocket's hit on its last cell), and by one for every hammer or rocket hit on the iced block itself. A rocket hit that takes one cell of a block is not a block gone. A block with ice left is locked; at zero it plays as any other block. Ice on doors is left out.
     - **Boosters**, in the slot order Freeze, Rocket, Hammer, Vacuum: Freeze stops the timer for 10 seconds and its button is off while the time is frozen; Rocket takes the tapped cell of an unlocked block and what remains splits into blocks of 4-connected cells (the middle of an L leaves two blocks); Hammer takes the tapped unlocked block entirely; on a locked block Rocket and Hammer take one ice instead. Vacuum takes every unlocked block of the tapped block's color, the locks read as they are at the tap; a locked block is not a Vacuum target.
     - **Aiming** (Rocket, Hammer, Vacuum): the screen dims except the board and the active button, a banner names the booster; only the Rocket shows crosshairs, one on every cell of every block, since it takes one cell (the Hammer takes the tapped block whole, the Vacuum every unlocked block of its color). A tap on a target fires; any other tap, or the button again, cancels; with no target every tap cancels. The time keeps running while aiming and firing. The stock goes down only when a shot lands.
     - **Stock**: each booster has a saved stock with a free starting amount. At zero its button opens a buy popup (name, icon, description, 3 for a price in coins; 1400 in the reference); the time stops while it is open; "watch an ad" is left out. Boosters are off before the level's first touch and once the level is decided.
   - Design:
     - **`Board` knows no mechanic.** It keeps the board's facts and geometry: its blocks (a list with room for one block per cell of the level, so a split appends without allocating; the hit block keeps its index for its first part), `RemainingBlocks`, `GoneCount` (raised in the one private removal path), `Hit` and `HitCell`, which give the hit to a guard first and remove only an unguarded block or cell. Mechanics plug in through two role interfaces next to it: `IMoveRule` (`Allows(board, block, direction)`, asked by `CanStep` and, with the outward direction, by `LeavesThrough`) and `IBlockGuard` (`Guards(board, block)`, `TakeHit(block)`, behind `Board.IsGuarded` and `Board.Hit`). Outside the attempt the board is shown by what may be done with it: `IReadOnlyBoard` for the roles, the views and target checks, `IBoard` (plus `Hit` and `HitCell`) for an effect's `Apply`.
     - **`IceMechanic`** (`Game.Logic`, `Play/Mechanics/Ice/`) plays both roles: it holds each block's starting ice and hits, and a block's ice left is the starting ice minus `GoneCount` minus its hits, never below zero. `BlockData.Ice` in the level format.
     - **`LevelMechanics.For(level)`** is the one place that builds the mechanics: it hands `Board` its rule and guard lists and the views a named reference (`Ice`, `Arrow`); the level attempt builds it with its board. The game, the level editor and the solver use it, so none can miss a mechanic. `LevelValidator` becomes a list of checks, ice adding `IceNeverMelts` (sorted from the smallest, the i-th block's ice must not exceed i).
     - **Booster effects in `Game.Logic` as commands**: `IBoardEffect` (`CanTarget(IReadOnlyBoard, cell)`, `Apply(IBoard, cell)`). Hammer: `Hit`; Rocket: `HitCell`; Vacuum: collect the unguarded blocks of the color (into a list allocated once), then `Hit` each. `LevelAttempt.TryApply(effect, x, y)` is the one way in (playing, aiming, a valid target, apply, the won check, aiming ends); `Aim` and `CancelAim` turn drag off and picking on without touching the timer; `CanUseBoosters`; `LevelTimer.Freeze(seconds)`, not used up while paused. Freeze is not an effect: it has no target.
     - **Stock and purchase in `Game.Logic`**, like `CoinWallet`: a `BoosterInventory` over a store saved through Core Save, `CoinWallet.TrySpend`, and a purchase that saves the stock before spending the coins.
     - **Boosters in `Game`** (`Play/Scripts/Boosters/`): `IBooster` (`Definition`, a settings asset with id, icon, name, description, starting stock, pack size and price; `IsAvailable(attempt)`; `Use(context, onDone)`). `TargetedBooster` fixes the order: aim, `TryApply`, stock down (saved), `Animate` (each booster's own), catch up, done. `FreezeBooster` implements `IBooster` directly. Registered by `PlayInstaller`; the HUD's slots are built from the list and read `IsAvailable` every frame. `PlaySession.Use(booster)` is the way in; its `Start` and `End` cancel any aim.
     - **Board touches go through modes**: `Drag` (today's), `Pick` (a tap gives a cell) and `Busy` (no touches while a shot plays, as the logic is already ahead of the views). One `CatchUp` brings every block view to the logic's state: ice left (a crack, a melt), shape (pieces rebuilt, a burst on the lost cell), new blocks (built from the pools), gone (a break). Whoever changes the board calls it; no event bus. Booster animations count in `IsSettled`, so a win waits for the last shot. A booster press releases a held block.
     - **Today's code to change**: `BoardInteraction.Leave` reads a block's length from `_level.Blocks[index]`, which has no entry for a split's new blocks; `BoardBuilder` builds block views only when it stages a level, `CatchUp` needs one at run time (`BlockPieceBuilder` from a list of cells).
     - **Art**: only the supplied booster buttons and icons; the stock badge from `Btn_circle_blue_small`, the buy popup from `PopupPanel` and `TextButtonGreen`, crosshairs from a tinted `SquareRoundedMask`, the rocket's flight from `Rocket_Purple_Icon`.
   - Work, in this order:
     1. **Groundwork**, one session on `develop` or a branch from it: `Board` with the block list, `GoneCount`, `Hit` and the role interfaces; `IceMechanic` and `LevelMechanics`; `IBoardEffect` and `LevelAttempt.TryApply` as the contract; the `CatchUp` skeleton; the `Leave` fix; the validator as a list of checks. Tests green, merged.
     2. **Three parallel sessions**, each in its own worktree and branch, each ending with a review: (A) ice: the validator check, the editor's ice field and drawing, the `IceCover` view, then the arrow block; (B) the booster flow: stock, purchase and popup, slots, the aiming overlay, touch modes, Freeze, Hammer, Vacuum; (C) the rocket: `HitCell` and the split, block views rebuilt and built at run time, then `RocketBooster` on B's flow once B is merged. A adds the ice child to the block prefab; C changes code only. Sessions leave `Plan.md` and `WorkLog.md` to the merges, which go B, C, A.
   - **Then the arrow block** (`Arrows.fbx` is supplied): a block that moves only along its arrow's axis, a second `IMoveRule`.
   - Then the solver (`Game.Logic`, engine-free, tests first), using the board's own step rules: moves are reversible and an exit only frees cells, so a level cannot be made unsolvable by the player and exiting a block as soon as it can is always safe; the search rearranges blocks (any cell a block can reach through free cells is one move) only when no block can exit. Gives an `Unsolvable` warning, the minimum move count and a solution the editor can play back step by step. A state budget keeps it bounded and reports "unknown" when exceeded. Its metrics (moves, rearrangement moves, dependency depth, blocks free at start) are the generator's input later.
   - Not asked by the case, after the above: shaped boards, the level generator, the out-of-time offer, ice on doors.
   - **Shaped boards** (the reference has them): boards that are not full rectangles, e.g. a notch cut into one side. The level format gains cells without ground inside the width and height, the board rules treat them as blocked, the level editor gets a tool to remove and restore cells, the validator checks that blocks and doors stand on the board's outline and that the board is one piece, and the frame follows the outline: walls along every edge between a cell and no cell, the rounded corner piece on outer corners and the supplied L and 2 x 2 corner pieces where the outline turns inward. The solver takes them through the board's own rules. Open: whether doors may sit on the inner edges of a notch.
   - Then a level generator in the editor (generate and test with a seeded `System.Random`: validator, solver, then difficulty bands from the solver's metrics; Easy, Medium and Hard profiles in a settings asset calibrated on the 5 levels; "similar to this level"; the result goes into the working copy for the designer to adjust), with static HTML reports from batch mode for tuning it (no HTML editor, no level-generation skill); the out-of-time offer, the first thing that spends coins (more seconds for coins: `CoinWallet.TrySpend`, `LevelTimer.AddSeconds`, an out-of-time state in `LevelAttempt` before `Failed`, a popup and a settings asset for price and seconds); then ice on doors (a door that opens after a number of blocks have gone, read from `GoneCount`).

## Risks
- Time: the case runs 7 days (2026-11-16 to 2026-11-22). Phases 4 and 5 (the required scope) are done; phase 7's ice blocker and boosters come before phase 6 at the developer's call, and phase 7 is ordered so that a cut falls at its end.
- The editor is the second most weighted criterion; budget real time for its usability, not only for its function.
- The live `unity command` bridge does not work on 2022.3; every editor action is a batch-mode run or an editor script, and batch mode cannot open the project while the editor has it open.
- The supplied models need per-file rotations and an own color set ([Supplied assets](SuppliedAssets.md)); budget phase 4 time for the block builder from quarter pieces.
